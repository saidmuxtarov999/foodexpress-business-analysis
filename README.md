# 🍔 FoodExpress - Business Analysis Repository

## 📌 Project Overview

FoodExpress is a food delivery platform designed to simplify the ordering process for customers, restaurants, couriers, and administrators.

This repository contains the complete Business Analysis documentation prepared during the analysis and design phase of the project.

---

# 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of FoodExpress is to digitize and optimize the food delivery process by providing:

- Easy food ordering
- Real-time order tracking
- Restaurant management
- Courier assignment
- Online payments
- Reporting & Analytics
